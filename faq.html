About DCUA

What is DCUA?

We use ultimate frisbee to help young people aged 12-20 build communication, leadership and critical thinking. In ultimate, players referee their own games, so these skills get practised in every game.

Does my child need experience?

No, beginners are very welcome. Every drill comes in three levels, from beginner up. Each player uses the level that suits them, so mixed groups work and nobody is left behind.

My child is 12. Will they play against 20-year-olds?

In games and drills against an opponent, players are paired by size and level. Every child gets game time, whatever their ability.

Are boys and girls mixed?

Yes. We run mixed groups, and girls are very welcome. Modest sportswear is welcome too.

What language are sessions in?

English.

Cost and membership

How much does it cost?

Ultimate Youth Project (UYP), our Saturday programme: RM300 per month. When two or more siblings join, each pays RM250 per month. Membership includes:

  • Weekly Saturday sessions, 9am–12pm at Taman Desa Park
  • Online coaching when scheduled, and training materials in our Discord group, an online chat app (for players aged 13+, with parental consent). If your child can't use Discord, we'll agree another way to get them the materials
  • Extra training sessions during school holidays when scheduled, at no extra cost
  • All discs and equipment
  • A monthly progress update for parents

Video reviews and 1-to-1 sessions are available on request, subject to Coach Kieran's availability.

Start with a free trial: sign up via the form. WhatsApp 017-312 1033 for questions, or if you need the link.

How and when do we pay?

Fees are paid by bank transfer, one month in advance. Each membership renews every month on its own payment date.

Do you give refunds?

Fees are non-refundable. Membership pays for our ongoing coaching, not for single sessions.

What if my child misses a session?

Missed sessions aren't refunded or made up one for one. Membership pays for our coaching as a whole. If we cancel a Saturday, for example for weather or haze, it isn't made up either. Holiday sessions and online coaching help your child keep up.

How do we cancel?

WhatsApp us before your next payment date. Your child can finish the month you've already paid for, with no partial refunds.

Can we pause, for example during exams?

Yes. Membership can be paused up to twice a year, for up to a month each time, around major exam periods. There's no fee for a paused month. WhatsApp us to arrange it.

Sessions

What happens in a session?

Every session has a warm-up, skill practice and games. We finish with a short group chat called the 'spirit circle', where the coach encourages players and invites them to share a reflection.

How do school programmes work?

For school programmes, a term runs for 12 weeks. Students learn the skills, practise them against opponents, then play full games. In week 12 there's a showcase: students lead the warm-up, play and reflect in front of parents.

Can I stay and watch?

Yes. Parents are always welcome to stay and watch.

Is it safe?

Ultimate is non-contact by rule. We collect parental consent and medical information before the first session, and there's a first-aid kit and water at every session.

My child has a medical condition.

Please tell us on the consent and medical form, which every family completes before the first session.

What if my child gets hurt?

The coach gives first aid from the kit we bring to every session, records what happened, and lets you know straight away.

Who is responsible for my child before and after sessions?

We're responsible during session hours and any coach-led extras, like a team lunch. Please drop off and pick up on time.

What happens in bad weather or haze?

We check the forecast and air quality, and confirm each Saturday session by Friday 6pm. If we have to cancel at short notice, we'll WhatsApp parents and players.

Can my child join partway through?

Yes. New players can join on any Saturday.

Progress

What will my child gain?

We teach, observe and report three outcomes: communication, leadership and critical thinking. We also watch for confidence, initiative and self-awareness. We observe and report each one, from "not yet visible" to "established".

How will I know how my child is doing?

UYP parents get a monthly progress update. If your child joins through their school, the school gets a summary report each term. The school can also ask for individual notes on each student.

Who is the coach?

Coach Kieran Ng, former international school teacher and coach (2019–2024). Coach Kieran also coaches Penyu, the unofficial Malaysian U20 team.

What's next for committed players?

Players will have the opportunity to compete in DC Ultimate Frisbee Academy events.
