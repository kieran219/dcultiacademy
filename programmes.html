// Session Structure

EVERY SESSION,
DESIGNED

Every session follows the same four blocks, so students know what to expect and every coach delivers the same standard. Each block builds a character skill as well as a sport skill.

01
Warm-Up & Alignment
Movement prep and an icebreaker that brings the group to the same energy. One student leads part of it. Builds communication.
02
Skill Block
Constraint-based drills that mix two or more fundamentals while spotlighting the week's focus skill. The drill design teaches the decision. Builds critical thinking.
03
Game Play
Small-sided games where students make their own calls, with one student given a specific captain's challenge. Builds leadership.
04
Spirit Circle
A short group chat where the coach encourages players and invites them to share a reflection.

// Course Structure

A 12-WEEK TERM,
FUNDAMENTALS FIRST

For school programmes, a term runs for 12 weeks. Students learn the skills, practise them against opponents, then play full games. In week 12 there's a showcase: students lead the warm-up, play and reflect in front of parents. Every term returns to four fundamentals and goes deeper.

Throwing
Flat backhands and forehands, arounds and uplines. Hucks for more proficient players.
Cutting
Jab cuts, lateral explosiveness, change of direction, timing and using space.
Catching
Catching at speed without slowing down, catching in space, high and low catches.
Strategy
Spacing, handler and cutter roles, reading the force and decisions within the stall count.
Weeks 1–4
Learn
The technique of each fundamental, without pressure.
Weeks 5–8
Pressure
The same skills against a marker or defender.
Weeks 9–12
Game
Full games. In week 12 there's a showcase: students lead the warm-up, play and reflect in front of parents.
// How progress is measured

We teach, observe and report three outcomes: communication, leadership and critical thinking. We also watch for confidence, initiative and self-awareness. We observe and report each one, from "not yet visible" to "established". UYP parents get a monthly progress update. If your child joins through their school, the school gets a summary report each term. The school can also ask for individual notes on each student.
